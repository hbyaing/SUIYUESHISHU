Ea VFX 0.2.0 — Forge 1.20.1 crimson cylinder beam prototype

Original effect implementation for this project. Vanilla iron-sword appearance is
referenced from Minecraft assets; no Minecraft texture or model binary is bundled.
The original beam prototype uses Fate / Ea as a visual reference.

Version 1.20.70 draw audio sources: the key and low descending tone were
synthesized for the mod. The flash and fracture use filtered transients
from the referenced Chinese dub of Fate/strange Fake: Whispers of Dawn,
with synthesized tails. The selections start at 42:34.490 (0.255 s) and
42:35.634 (0.320 s) in the reference video.
https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1BkZiBkETv/

beam_flow.png is original, procedurally authored packed noise/filament/pulse data.
Its deterministic generator is included in verification/generate_beam_texture.py.

Framebuffer/state handling is adapted from the local Energy VFX Workbench
reference (MIT, Copyright 2026 Energy VFX Workbench contributors).
